# Business brief

## Scenario

The growth team receives channel reports that optimize attributed ROAS, but finance questions whether paid media is creating new demand. Leadership needs a common measurement layer before the next quarterly budget cycle.

## Stakeholders

- VP Growth: channel contribution and growth trade-offs
- Performance Marketing: campaign diagnostics and pacing
- Finance: incremental revenue and spend controls
- Analytics: experiment validity, reusable metrics, and caveats

## Deliverable

Build a reproducible mart and decision dashboard that compares attributed and incremental performance, identifies uncertainty, and demonstrates one auditable fixed-budget scenario.

## Acceptance criteria

- Treatment and control remain traceable from source to dashboard.
- Every displayed KPI has an inspectable formula in SQL or Python.
- The allocation conserves total spend and applies explicit constraints.
- The presentation separates historical estimates from recommendations.
